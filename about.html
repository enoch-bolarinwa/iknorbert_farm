Our Story

Three generations of farming, rooted in the same soil.

How We Started

Green Acres Farm began in 1991 when the Harmon family converted a small hay field into a vegetable garden to feed their neighbors. What started as a roadside stand has grown into a working farm that supplies local markets, restaurants, and families across the valley.

We still farm the same way we did on day one: no synthetic pesticides, careful crop rotation, and a lot of hands-on work from sunrise to sunset.

What We Believe

Sustainability

We rotate crops, compost our own waste, and keep chemical inputs out of the ground.

Community

We sell directly to our neighbors whenever we can, keeping food dollars local.

Animal Welfare

Our hens and goats have room to roam, fresh pasture, and clean water, always.
